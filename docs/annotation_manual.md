# Annotation manual

## Goal

Identify the provider and service supported by evidence valid at the observation time and known at the decision time.

## Required labels

1. Observable issue class distribution
2. Location and uncertainty
3. Candidate asset identifiers
4. Candidate providers and services
5. Selected provider and service or unresolved state
6. Supporting and contradicting evidence
7. Duplicate relation
8. Escalation path
9. Minimum sufficient evidence

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer dated official responsibility records, asset inventories, permit obligations, contracts, and service definitions. Treat historical request agency fields as weak evidence.

## Unresolved labels

Use an unresolved label when:

- Two providers remain plausible
- The exact asset cannot be identified
- A private or utility owner is not supported by public records
- The relevant historical service definition is missing
- Evidence conflicts without a documented precedence rule

## Duplicate labels

Choose one:

- Same physical defect
- Same asset with a different defect
- Recurring defect after closure
- Overlapping construction issue
- Stale resolved case
- Not a duplicate

Proximity alone is not sufficient.

## Adjudication

Annotators must record the evidence span that drives each field. The adjudicator resolves disagreements from the preserved records and may choose unresolved. Agreement should be reported separately for provider, service, duplicate relation, and minimum sufficient evidence.
